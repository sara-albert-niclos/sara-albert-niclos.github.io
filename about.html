About

I am a mathematician and researcher working in geometric analysis, with a particular interest in geometric flows and the interplay between geometry, analysis, and partial differential equations.

My research focuses on geometric flows with global constraints. I am particularly interested in how these constraints influence the geometry and long-time behaviour of the flow, and in the phenomena that arise when classical geometric properties are no longer preserved.I am also interested in inpainting problems in the setting of Riemannian manifolds, which I plan to explore further.
