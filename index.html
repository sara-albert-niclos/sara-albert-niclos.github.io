01 / About

About

I am a mathematician working in geometric analysis and geometric flows.

I am currently a PhD researcher in Mathematics, interested in the study of geometric evolution equations and curvature-driven phenomena.

My research focuses on understanding how geometric quantities evolve under constrained curvature flows, with particular attention to global properties and geometric inequalities.

02 / Research

Research

My research lies in geometric analysis, with a particular focus on geometric evolution equations, curvature flows and the interaction between local curvature and global constraints.

01

Curvature flows

Curve Shortening Flow, Mean Curvature Flow and related geometric evolution equations.

02

Global constraints

Area-, length- and volume-preserving flows, and the role of global geometric quantities.

03

Geometric analysis

Geometric inequalities, Riemannian geometry and flows on curved ambient spaces.
