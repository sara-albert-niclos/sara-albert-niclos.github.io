About

I am a mathematician and researcher working in geometric analysis, with a particular interest in geometric flows and the interplay between geometry, analysis, and partial differential equations.

My research focuses on the evolution of geometric objects under curvature-driven flows with global constraints. I am particularly interested in these constraints influence the geometry and long-time behaviour of these evolutions, and in the phenomena that arise when classical geometric properties are no longer preserved.

Research

My research lies in geometric analysis, with a particular focus on geometric evolution equations, curvature flows and the interaction between local curvature and global constraints.

Curvature flows

Curve Shortening Flow, Mean Curvature Flow and related geometric evolution equations.

Global constraints

Area-, length- and volume-preserving flows, and the role of global geometric quantities.

Geometric analysis

Geometric inequalities, Riemannian geometry and flows on curved ambient spaces.
